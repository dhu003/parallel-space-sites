Where the numbers come from

Loan rate 10% — SBA 7(a) typical: prime 7.00% + 3.0% max spread for loans over $350K (Fed H.15 / SBA, Oct 2026). Re-check after the Dec 8–9 FOMC meeting.

Growth 4%/yr — long-run nominal GDP growth, about 3.8–4.1% (CBO, Feb 2026).

Tax 25% — US average combined federal + state corporate rate, 25.5% (Tax Foundation, 2026). C-corp basis; pass-through owners pay individual rates.

Closing costs 2% — typical deal costs run 1–4% of price before financing fees (legal + diligence 1–3%).

Equity 15% — SBA 7(a) minimum is 10% (5% from your own funds); 15% is a typical buyer contribution.

Working capital — receivables, payables and inventory terms are researched per industry (e.g. trucking collects in ~40 days, car wash ~0, general contractor ~75 days plus retainage) — see the working-capital assumptions in each preset.

Industry defaults — each preset’s “Current model” note names its benchmark (BizBuySell sold listings, NAHB, ATRI, IBBA). Scale tiers carry their own sources in the model note above.

Decision-use note: This free calculator is an educational planning tool, not investment, accounting or tax advice. Validate assumptions and deal terms with qualified advisers before relying on the output.
